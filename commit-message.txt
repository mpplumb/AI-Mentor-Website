Inner pages: section patterns, cream CTAs, lit colour bands

All four inner pages (AI Overview, Strategy, Services, Contact)
rebuilt on a shared set of section patterns. Homepage untouched.

Section patterns
- Four reusable layouts introduced: editorial split with a figure
  panel, multi-column card rows, a yes/no comparison table, and a
  glass panel over an animated mesh
- Each page uses the glass panel exactly once as its feature moment
- Seven legacy per-section CSS blocks removed; they were beating the
  new patterns on ID specificity and forcing flat black grounds

Calls to action
- Cream button with a warm glow now carries every inner-page CTA
- AI Overview gained a CTA it did not have
- Button wording set in title case: Learn More, Start With A Call,
  Get A Quote, Send An Inquiry

Section grounds
- Sections alternate tinted green and blue bands, drawn from the
  logo hues (#E3FAEE and #D2ECFE) rather than neutral greys
- Each band is lit rather than flat: a radial bloom in the logo
  colour from one top corner, a quieter counter-bloom in the other
  hue from the opposite bottom corner, and a feathered white sheen
  along the top edge
- The lit corner alternates between green and blue bands, so light
  appears to travel down the page
- Blue bands run brighter than green by design; body text clears
  WCAG AA on both (5.17:1 on the blue)

Verified at 430-1920px: no horizontal overflow, no console errors.
